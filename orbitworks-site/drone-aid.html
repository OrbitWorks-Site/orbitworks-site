What We Do

Drone Aid Mission Capabilities

🚁
Supply Drop Missions
Rapid delivery of critical supplies — medical kits, food, communication equipment — to areas inaccessible by ground due to conflict or terrain.
📡
Situational Awareness
ISR support for NGOs and aid teams. Real-time aerial imagery to improve operational awareness, route planning, and safety for aid workers on the ground.
🏥
Medical Logistics
Delivering medical supplies, blood, and medications to field hospitals and forward aid stations. Time-critical deliveries that conventional transport cannot make.
🔍
Search & Rescue Support
Thermal and RGB aerial search support for missing persons, survivors in rubble, and disaster zone assessment. Works alongside ground SAR teams.
📊
Damage Assessment
Post-disaster photogrammetry and mapping for damage assessment, resource allocation planning, and recovery prioritization.
🤝
Partner Organization Support
We work directly with humanitarian groups and government agencies as a drone capability extension — providing hardware, pilots, and mission planning support.
