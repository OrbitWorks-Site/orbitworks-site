About Us

Built for the Mission

OrbitWorks Aerospace Inc. is a drone technology company based in Vestal, NY — building commercial UAS systems, running humanitarian drone operations, and developing advanced aerospace defense systems.

Our Story

From Field Research to Full-Spectrum UAS

OrbitWorks was founded with a single mission: put capable, reliable drone technology in the hands of the operators who need it most — whether that's a filmmaker, a surveyor, a farmer, or a humanitarian organization in a crisis zone.

Our founder conducted field research observing drone warfare and humanitarian operations from 2022–2024, giving us a ground-level understanding of what works and what doesn't when lives are on the line.

That experience drives everything we build — rugged, reliable systems with real-world validation behind them.
