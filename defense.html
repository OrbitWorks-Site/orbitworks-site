Allied Nation Partnerships

OrbitWorks Aerospace is actively seeking partnerships with allied foreign nations in areas including manufacturing cooperation and technological development. The counter-UAS threat is global — addressing it requires collaboration across borders with trusted partners who share our commitment to security and innovation.

We welcome inquiries from government and defense industry representatives of FMS-eligible nations interested in exploring cooperative opportunities. All engagements are conducted in full compliance with U.S. export control regulations, including ITAR and EAR.
