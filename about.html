Our Story

From Space Systems to Advanced Defense Technology

OrbitWorks Aerospace was founded in February 2021 with a vision to build advanced space systems. When Russia invaded Ukraine in 2022, the company pivoted — recognizing that the most urgent need in modern warfare was affordable, capable drone technology and the systems to counter it.

From 2022 through 2025, OrbitWorks developed prototypes and delivered FPV drones, transmitters, controllers, and accessories to support Ukrainian defense efforts — funded through NAFO fundraising on social media and in-person events in Washington, D.C. and Binghamton, NY. Through a close partnership with Ukrainian frontline soldiers, OrbitWorks received signal readouts on enemy drones and electronic warfare systems, telemetrics, performance statistics, and critical operational data that no lab or simulation can replicate.

That partnership drove the development of newer, more robust systems — including non-armed Talon interceptors that were deployed and used on the battlefield by Ukrainian partners with remarkable success. In 2026, years of real-world data and iterative development culminated in the Vanguard SHORAD counter-UAS system — a domestic, cost-effective answer to the drone threats posed by Russia, Iran, and China.

The next step: a Tier I manufacturing facility to produce these systems at scale — right here in the United States.
