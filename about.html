Binghamton Makers Building Better Drones

OrbitWorks Aerospace Inc. is a hands-on drone workshop in Binghamton, NY — designing, assembling, and flight-testing platforms for photography, FPV, mapping, education, and commercial ops.

Our Story

From Aerospace Curiosity to a Local Drone Shop

OrbitWorks Aerospace was founded in February 2021 with a passion for aerospace technology. What started as space-systems curiosity quickly became a practical drone workshop — building airframes, integrating electronics, and helping people get airborne for real jobs and real hobbies.

Today we focus on civilian and commercial platforms: cinema and photography rigs, FPV kits for freestyle and racing, mapping and survey systems, custom builds, Parts 107–ready training, and STEM outreach for local schools. Every platform is assembled and tested in Binghamton with US-sourced components.

We also support specialized defense programs for qualified government and contractor partners — see the Defense Portal for those inquiries.

Next up: expanding our workshop capacity so more makers, crews, and commercial operators can fly US-built gear.
